A fast first read before an expensive one

Most applicants don’t know whether they’re ready until they’re deep in the application, or paying a solicitor to find out. ILR-Calc gives a structured first read: ready to apply, fixable issues to resolve first, or a blocker that needs attention.

Your answers never leave your browser

ILR-Calc collects no personally identifying information: no name, no email, no account, and no tracking.

Anyone can open the browser’s network tab and confirm that no tracking or analytics calls leave their machine. That is why it’s safe to use with sensitive immigration details.
