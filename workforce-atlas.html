Workforce Atlas: AI workforce audits for professional firms.

Atlas is an audit method and the service that delivers it. We map your team’s time, find the repetitive work that uses up fee-earner capacity, and design the small set of AI employees to deploy alongside your people.

One method, two ways to engage

The audit

A structured audit with a workforce map, a ranked list of opportunities, and a recommended AI workforce.

The delivery

We design, deploy, train, and maintain the AI workforce for you, on a managed retainer that follows the audit.

What’s in an audit

  • A workforce map of where your team’s time goes today, across nine workflow areas
  • The top five automation opportunities, ranked by return and complexity
  • A recommended AI workforce, typically four to six AI employees sized to your firm
  • A 90-day deployment roadmap and a managed retainer to deliver it
  • One fixed principle: capacity unlocked, never headcount reduced

How we deliver it

Atlas runs on our AI embedment model: one senior engineer-operator embedded in your business, who builds the AI workforce alongside your team and runs it until your team is ready to take over.

Atlas unlocks capacity. We design AI workforces that take the repetitive work off your team so the team becomes more valuable. If the goal is layoffs, Atlas is the wrong tool.
