Three kinds of engagement, each led by someone who has done the job.

We scope before we price. Most clients start with one engagement and add another once the first is working.

Transformation programmes

For a function that needs to reach a defined future state, where the programme has lost momentum. You keep the new operating model and a team that runs it.

Usually 2–5 days a week, on a monthly retainer.

Operations leadership

For an operation that works at today’s volume and will break at the next one. We rebuild the model, tooling, process, and team so it scales.

Project-based, or fractional.

AI embedment

For AI that has a strategy and a pilot but no production system. We take it into production with compliance designed in, and stay until your team owns it.

How an engagement runs

Four phases, the same on every engagement. You approve each one before the next starts.

Discover

Week 1

We listen, read, sit with the team, and ask the hard questions.

Design

Weeks 2–3

We map the path. You sign it off before we build.

Deliver

Week 4 onwards

Weekly demos and weekly notes, so you always know where things stand.

Hand back

At the end

We document, train your team, and step out. The capability stays with you.
