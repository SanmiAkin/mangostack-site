Cash arrives. The work begins.

Payments land in your bank account with bad references, or none at all. One transfer covers five invoices. A customer short-pays without explanation. Somebody on the finance team works through them line by line, matching incoming cash to open invoices, posting allocations, and chasing what won’t add up.

UK businesses are owed an estimated £26 billion in late payments at any time, and chasing it takes roughly 86 hours per business a year. The hours are only part of the cost. The cash position lags reality, credit control gets pushed aside for reconciling, and month-end drags because the AR ledger isn’t clean.

The pattern. A finance team that knows how to apply cash, doing it manually for the hundredth time this month.

How Pipworth works

Connect your bank and Sage

Secure Open Banking on one side, read-only ledger access where possible on the other. Setup takes an afternoon.

Pipworth reads the data

Incoming payments from your bank feed. Open invoices, customers, and history from your ledger.

It matches them

Exact references, combined invoices, partial payments, and the settlement patterns each customer has used before. Every match comes with its reasoning.

You approve, or let it post

Your choice, per the mode you set. Pipworth handles the routine and your team handles the judgement calls.

You decide how much to hand over

Start cautious and scale as you trust it. You can change the mode whenever you want, per customer or across the ledger.

Suggest

Pipworth proposes every match and your team approves each one. Full control.

Hybrid

Questions

Does it work with my version of Sage?

Sage 200 and Sage Intacct are supported today. We’ll confirm your specific version on the demo call.

How long does setup take?

An afternoon to connect the bank and the ledger. Two weeks to be live in your chosen mode, with rules tuned to your customers’ payment patterns.

Is my financial data safe?

Open Banking is FCA-regulated and Pipworth never sees your bank login. Ledger access is read-only wherever possible, write-back follows your rules, and your data stays in the UK.

What happens when Pipworth isn’t sure about a match?

It tells you. The match goes to your team with the reasoning and the alternatives, and a person makes the call.

Do I lose control of my ledger?

You set the thresholds, choose the mode, and decide what posts and what escalates. The finance team owns the ledger.

What size of business is this for?

Mid-market finance teams on Sage, applying anywhere from hundreds to tens of thousands of payments a month.
