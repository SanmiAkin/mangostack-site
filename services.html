Three kinds of engagement

Pick one or combine them. Every engagement is led by a senior operator who has done the job before.

Transformation programmes

We take a function from where it is to a defined future state, with accountability for the outcome. That covers embedded leadership for organisation, process, or technology change: programme governance, multi-market rollouts, stakeholder management, and vendor and partner orchestration.

Usually 2–5 days a week, on a monthly retainer.

Operations leadership

We run or rebuild an operation so it scales. The model, tooling, process, and team are designed, instrumented, and AI-augmented where it earns its keep, and built to run after we leave.

Project-based, or fractional.

AI embedment

We move AI from intention to production: a strategy that ends in something shipped, integration with compliance designed in, and adoption that lasts after the launch demo. Where software needs building, a senior engineer-operator builds it inside your team.

Usually 3–5 days a week for 3–6 months.

How an engagement runs

Four phases, the same on every engagement. You approve each one before the next starts.

Discover

Week 1. We listen, read, sit with the team, and ask the hard questions.

Design

Weeks 2–3. We map the path. You sign it off before we build.

Deliver

Week 4 onwards. Weekly demos and weekly notes, so you always know where things stand.

Hand back

At the end. We document, train your team, and step out. The capability stays with you.
